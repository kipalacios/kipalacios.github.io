my_story:
  - heading: My Creative Origins
    body: >
      My path to communications didn’t begin in a newsroom or a creative studio—it began with an
      interest in law, policy, and the structures that shape power. I was drawn to the courtroom as
      a site of consequence, where language carries weight and stories determine outcomes. As I was
      immersed further into the legal realm, I realized my curiosity was less about legal procedure
      and more about what came before it: the narratives, perceptions, and social forces that bring
      people there in the first place.
  - heading: Shifting the Lens
    body: >
      Living with a vision disability ultimately reshaped that trajectory. It required me to
      reconsider not only how I worked, but where my strengths were most impactful. In stepping away
      from a traditional legal path, I found clarity in communications—a field where strategy,
      storytelling, and empathy intersect, and where accessibility and perspective are not
      afterthoughts but essential components of effective messaging.
  - heading: Building With Intention
    body: >
      Today, my work sits at the intersection of PR, advertising, research, and cultural analysis. I
      approach campaigns the way I once approached policy questions: by asking who is being heard,
      who is being excluded, and how language can be used responsibly and creatively to bridge that
      gap. My academic background in political communication and legal history continues to inform
      my work, grounding creative ideas in context, nuance, and intention.
  - heading: Rewriting My Narrative
    body: >
      At its core, my story is about reframing—seeing communication not just as promotion, but as a
      tool for understanding, connection, and change. It’s this lens that shapes how I collaborate,
      create, and build work that feels both thoughtful and human.

Creativity and storytelling have always shaped the way I see and engage with the world—from crafting narratives and melodies to photography, social media, and other forms of multimedia expression.

I believe the best stories aren't just told—they're experienced. That means creating work that invites people in through more than one medium, perspective, or point of entry. I'm passionate about accessible storytelling as a way to reach audiences across backgrounds and abilities, using different forms of media to make ideas more engaging, inclusive, and meaningful.

My perspective is shaped by a vision disability that has taught me to approach communication through a different lens: one grounded in accessibility, empathy, and creative problem-solving. Today, I bring that perspective to public relations, advertising, and creative strategy, creating work that doesn't simply capture attention, but gives people different ways to see, feel, and connect with a story.

Create more than you consume. Tell stories people can experience.
