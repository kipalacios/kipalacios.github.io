  - title: "Aluminate LA &mdash; Recycling Program with the Los Angeles Chargers"
    meta: "With the Los Angeles Chargers &amp; Ball Corporation &middot; October 2023 &ndash; April 2024"
    summary: >
      Sponsored by Ball Corporation, the Los Angeles Chargers partnered with the Youth Development
      Department to challenge Youth Council members to design a campaign promoting aluminum recycling.
      The winning team's idea, Aluminate LA, brings artist-designed recycling bins to all 15 council
      districts, and launched at Los Angeles City Hall.
    role: >
      I saw the campaign from start to finish, including each of the events. As a Community
      Administrative Support Worker for the Youth Development Department, I supported the community
      engagement team beyond the recruitment of the Youth Council: I captured content from site visits
      at SoFi Stadium, coordinated with the Los Angeles Chargers communications staff in pushing out
      this content on socials, and was the project manager for the Youth Council's project pitches to
      the LA Chargers team. This included briefing presentations for each of the 4 teams before the
      final presentation to the LA Chargers team and City Council.
