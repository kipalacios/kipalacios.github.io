overview:
  assignment: >
    A co-branded promotional campaign imagining a partnership between CAVA and Bumble — the
    brand copy, a paid social set, and the out-of-home creative to launch it.
  idea: >
    Find Your Flavor ties CAVA's build-your-own bowl philosophy to Bumble's build-your-own
    connection pitch — positioning both brands around a shared belief that the best
    combinations, in food and in people, come from trying something new.
  product: >
    The Perfect Match Bowl — a limited-edition CAVA x Bumble creation with roasted sweet
    potatoes, spicy harissa honey chicken, feta, crispy chickpeas, and a drizzle of garlic
    dressing — alongside a Bumble-exclusive Buy One Bowl, Share One Free offer running through
    December 31.

story:
  - title: Find Your Flavor
    img: assets/img/portfolio/cava-bumble/lifestyle-01.jpg
    alt: Two friends walking with a co-branded CAVA x Bumble bag reading "Find Your Flavor"
    body: >
      Life's better when you mix it up — whether that means trying a new ingredient, a new
      combo, or meeting someone new. That's why this season, CAVA and Bumble are coming together
      to help you Find Your Flavor — a celebration of taste, chemistry, and connection.
  - body: >
      At CAVA, we believe food should be personal. No two bowls are ever the same — and that's
      the beauty of it. You can swap your grains for greens, trade your tzatziki for hummus, or
      add a little extra harissa honey chicken when you're feeling bold. Every combination tells
      a story about who you are and what you're craving in the moment.
  - body: >
      Bumble believes the same thing about people. Every conversation, every swipe, every
      connection is an opportunity to explore something new — a friendship, a spark, a moment
      that might surprise you. Together, we're celebrating that spirit of curiosity, because
      great taste isn't just about food — it's about trying, choosing, and discovering what
      (and who) makes life a little more flavorful.
  - title: Match. Meet. Eat. Repeat.
    img: assets/img/portfolio/cava-bumble/poster-flavor.jpg
    alt: CAVA x Bumble "Find Your Flavor" poster with two friends sharing bowls
    body: >
      From now through December 31, Bumble users can unlock exclusive CAVA offers right in the
      app — like Buy One Bowl, Share One Free rewards that make meeting up IRL easier (and
      tastier) than ever. Whether it's a first date, a friend date, or a casual "let's grab
      lunch," we've got your table covered.
  - body: >
      And for those ready to test their taste buds, meet the Perfect Match Bowl — our
      limited-edition CAVA x Bumble creation. It's got roasted sweet potatoes, spicy harissa
      honey chicken, feta, crispy chickpeas, and a drizzle of garlic dressing — sweet, spicy,
      balanced, and full of chemistry. Basically, it's what happens when two great flavors
      finally match.
  - body: >
      So go ahead — swipe right on something new. Trade your bowl for a pita, mix up your dips,
      share a meal, or meet your match. Because at CAVA, we know one thing for sure: when you
      combine bold flavors and good company, you'll always find something worth coming back for.
